WHAT WE DO
Three ways to work with us.
01
Agent-first delivery
We build your product or internal tooling the way we work ourselves: agents carrying the repetitive 80%, humans on the judgment calls. You get working software — and the workflow that produced it.
02
Agentic workflows & orchestration
We map your processes and rebuild them as multi-agent pipelines — with the right handoffs, guardrails, and human review gates, so the automation is one you can actually trust.
03
AI education
Structured training from beginner to expert: prompting fundamentals, daily AI workflows, and advanced agent orchestration — taught on your real work, not toy demos.
PROCESS
From audit to autonomy.
01 / AUDIT
We sit with your team and find where agents actually fit — and where they don't.
02 / PILOT
One workflow, rebuilt agent-first and put into production. Small enough to ship fast, real enough to matter.
03 / TRAIN
Your people learn to run, review, and extend the system — at whatever level they start from.
04 / SCALE
More workflows, less of us. The goal is a team that doesn't need consultants.
